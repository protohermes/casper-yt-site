1. Overview

Casper YT (“Casper,” “the application,” “we,” or “us”) is a private, owner-operated application used to prepare, upload, and schedule video content on the owner's YouTube channel. It is not offered as a public consumer service and does not create public user accounts.

2. Google data accessed

Casper uses Google OAuth 2.0 and requests the youtube.upload permission. This permission lets the application upload videos and associated publishing metadata to the YouTube channel authorized by the owner.

The application receives OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain that authorization. Casper does not request the owner's Google password and does not use Google data for advertising or profiling.

3. How data is used

Google authorization data is used solely to:

  • Authenticate upload requests made by the owner-operated workflow.
  • Upload video files, titles, descriptions, tags, and scheduling settings selected by the owner.
  • Renew OAuth access when necessary so scheduled publishing can continue.

4. Storage, security, and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in restricted private storage on the owner's server and are not included in this public website or source repository. Reasonable technical safeguards are used to restrict access.

Credentials are retained only while the owner uses the application. They are deleted when authorization is revoked or when the application is discontinued, except where temporary retention is required for security, legal compliance, or backup rotation.

5. Data sharing

Casper does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Data may be processed by Google and YouTube as necessary to provide OAuth and video-upload functionality, or disclosed if required by applicable law.

6. Your choices and control

The channel owner can revoke Casper's Google access at any time from the Google Account permissions page. To request deletion of credentials associated with this application, contact us using the email below.

7. Google API Services disclosure

Casper YT's use and transfer to any other application of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or legal obligations change. The latest effective date will always appear at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions, revocation assistance, or data-deletion requests can be sent to protohermes@outlook.com.
